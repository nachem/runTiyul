# RunTiyul v1.3.0

Release status: [published 2026-07-27](https://github.com/nachem/runTiyul/releases/tag/v1.3.0)<br>
App version: `1.3.0+8`

RunTiyul v1.3.0 makes audio navigation actionable when a trail runner leaves
the route and adds configurable, low-interruption progress guidance while the
runner remains on course.

## Highlights

### Directional route recovery

- Repeats off-route guidance at a configurable 10-60 second interval instead of
  warning only once.
- Reports the nearest-route distance and compass direction. While the runner has
  a reliable moving course, guidance also says whether the route is ahead, left,
  right, or behind.
- Uses a compact tone vocabulary that works without speech: one initial warning,
  two slow warning cues when approaching the route, and three fast warning cues
  when moving away.
- Keeps stationary guidance compass-based rather than guessing which way the
  runner is facing.

### On-route progress guidance

- Announces completed and remaining route distance at configurable 0.5-5 km
  intervals or 5-60 minute intervals.
- Keeps progress monotonic through GPS jitter and suppresses progress prompts
  while off route.
- Gives off-route and junction events priority when they coincide with a
  progress milestone.
- Uses two relaxed rising cues and a light haptic for progress, distinct from the
  heavy off-route warning and medium junction haptic.

### Configurable previews

- Adds Route finder and Progress previews beside the existing Off route and
  Junction previews.
- Persists reminder cadence, progress enablement, distance/time mode, and the
  selected interval with the existing Tone + voice, Voice, Tones, and Haptics
  only output modes.
- Voice-only mode falls back to the corresponding complete tone pattern when an
  offline English system voice is unavailable.

## Known Limitations

- Tone audibility outdoors, Bluetooth/open-ear routing, moving-course quality,
  and background/locked-screen playback still require physical Android and iOS
  verification.
- The `v1.2.2` to `v1.3.0` in-place Android upgrade is enabled by the permanent
  signing contract but still needs a physical-device data-preservation test.
- Visual route-progress percentage and course-up presentation remain
  unimplemented.
- Production offline-map provider approval and iOS runtime verification remain
  unresolved.
