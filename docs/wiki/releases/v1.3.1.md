# RunTiyul v1.3.1

Release status: prepared 2026-08-15<br>
App version: `1.3.1+9`

RunTiyul v1.3.1 fixes navigation prompts leaving music paused after an alert
finishes.

## Highlights

### Music resumes after navigation prompts

- Releases transient audio focus after the final navigation tone or spoken
  prompt so the interrupted music app can continue playback.
- Stops Android low-latency SoundPool cues after their known duration because
  that backend does not provide a playback-completion callback.
- Deactivates the iOS shared audio session with
  `notifyOthersOnDeactivation`, allowing the interrupted audio app to resume.
- Keeps alert ownership generation-aware so a delayed older alert cannot stop
  or release a newer prompt.
- Preserves all existing Tone + voice, Voice, Tones, Haptics only, cadence,
  fallback, and offline behavior.

## Downloads and Installation

Stable downloads become available from the
[RunTiyul website](https://nachem.github.io/runTiyul/) and GitHub Releases after
the release workflow completes.

| Platform | File | Installation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android | `RunTiyul.apk` | Builds installed from permanently signed `v1.2.2` or later can update in place. Builds from `v1.2.0` or older still require the documented one-time uninstall. |

## Known Limitations

- Music resumption after navigation prompts still requires physical Android and
  iOS verification with representative music and podcast apps, Bluetooth or
  open-ear headphones, and background or locked-screen guidance.
- iOS runtime and unsigned IPA sideloading remain unverified.
- Tone audibility outdoors, moving-course quality, and background GPS recording
  still require physical-device verification.
- Production offline-map provider approval remains unresolved.
