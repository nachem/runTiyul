# RunTiyul v1.4.5

Release status: [published 2026-09-23](https://github.com/nachem/runTiyul/releases/tag/v1.4.5)<br>
App version: `1.4.5+15`

Checkpoint routes now show their routed preview before saving and search more
nearby trails before falling back to a direct connection.

## Changes

- **RTE-003/RTE-011:** checkpoint pathfinding considers up to 16 nearby
  candidates within 150 m and follows long mapped bends without applying
  recorded-track chord limits. Fully mapped alternatives take priority.
- Points within 40 m snap to a nearby way. More distant checkpoints retain
  their positions and can reach nearby ways through explicitly unmapped
  approach segments. Close switchback checkpoints remain distinct.
- Planning can bridge a plausible same-level fragment gap of at most 12 m,
  reporting only the gap as unmapped. These connectors are penalized and are
  never made available to strict navigation recovery as mapped edges.
- Missing connections trigger one wider corridor search within the existing
  256-tile budget. Offline mode remains cache/local-only.
- **RTE-009/RTE-012:** new snapping-enabled Checkpoints routes preview their
  geometry during selection. Original taps are retained through draft edits,
  control insertion, and Undo. Save preserves the exact displayed geometry
  without a second background snap.
- Saved manual routes use checkpoint pathfinding; imported GPX routes retain
  shape-constrained matching and metadata protection.

## Limitations and Licensing

- Approach, gap, and direct segments are user-planned geometry, not verified
  walkable paths or permission to cross barriers or private land. Map coverage,
  access information, and bounded searches cannot guarantee a best real-world
  route. Long-route phone performance and outdoor behavior remain unverified.
- Raw checkpoints and per-segment diagnostics remain draft-only. Saved route
  geometry persists, but reopening does not reconstruct the original taps.
- Provider permissions, dependencies, schema, signing identity, and location
  privacy are unchanged. Public builds retain ordinary provider gates; Esri
  offline downloads still require a separately authorized DEV build and
  on-device confirmation. No new production offline rights are granted.
- No physical-device installation, data-preserving upgrade, or iOS runtime
  validation has been performed for this release.
