# RunTiyul v1.4.4

Release status: publication requested on 2026-09-23; builds pending<br>
App version: `1.4.4+14`

Route planning follows mapped bends more reliably and keeps distant points
as explicit direct connections instead of rejecting the edit.

## Changes

- **RTE-003/RTE-011:** sparse waypoints can follow a mapped right-angle or
  winding path without treating the straight chord as recorded geometry.
  Same-level branch endpoints connect at segment interiors; nearby parallel
  ways and grade-separated crossings remain separate.
- Nearby points snap within 40 m. Planning compares nearby candidates to
  prefer a connected path over an isolated fragment. Distant points stay at
  their chosen locations and connect directly when no reasonable mapped path
  is available. The editor reports direct, unmapped segments; navigation
  recovery still requires strictly mapped connections.
- **RTE-009/RTE-012:** local editing and Undo preserve unaffected geometry;
  repeated taps on the same snapped point do not add duplicate controls.
  Whole-route snapping retains off-map stops and matching GPX metadata, and
  reports when direct connections remain.
- **OFF-012:** the tappable current-map picker reaches the developer
  confirmation flow again and displays the resolved `DEV` source after unlock.
  VS Code includes a separate **Running App (authorized raster DEV)** profile
  for separately permitted Esri World Imagery and Topographic testing.
  Ordinary builds retain their provider restrictions.

## Limitations and Licensing

- Direct connections are user-planned geometry, not verified walkable paths
  or permission to cross private land. Their editor diagnostic is transient,
  not persisted per segment; saved geometry itself remains intact.
- Display-vector data can omit topology, access metadata, and paths. Endpoint
  joins require coordinate-level coincidence; arbitrary crossing lines are
  not inferred as junctions. Long-route device performance remains unverified.
- Esri offline use requires separate permission and the authorized DEV build
  flag plus on-device confirmation. No new production-provider rights are
  granted; attribution, tile limits, pacing, and cooldowns remain enforced.
- Dependencies, schema, signing identity, and location privacy are unchanged.
